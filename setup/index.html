Get started in a minute

Four steps from invite to your first finished task.

  1. Add the botInvite Task Assigner to your server with the Add to Discord button.
  2. Open settingsRun /settings to manage groups, rules and loggers. Admins only.
  3. Set the rulesCreate groups and decide who can assign to whom.
  4. Assign a taskRun /assign, pick people or groups, a priority and a due date.

Questions

Quick answers before you add the bot.

Does Task Assigner use slash commands?

Yes. Every command works as a slash command and as a prefix command. Admins can manage prefixes with /prefix.

Who can assign tasks to whom?

Admins decide in /settings. Make groups from people and roles, let group A assign to group B or to one person, add exceptions, and block people, roles or @everyone from assigning. Unassign rules work the same way.

Can people say no to a task?

Assignees get Accept and Reject buttons in a DM or the channel, or tasks can be accepted directly, depending on how your server is set up.

How do reminders work?

Due reminders can be turned on or off with one button. You get a heads-up an hour before and a nudge when the task is due.

How do I snooze a reminder?

Use the Snooze button on a reminder or run /snooze with any length such as 30m, 2h, 1d 6h or 1w.

Where do logs go?

Loggers can send assigned, progress and completed logs to a channel, a person or a whole group. A test button shows exactly what was delivered.

Is my data sold?

No. We don't sell your data. The Privacy Policy explains what is stored and why.

Where can I get help?

Message @armaansidhu223 on Discord.
