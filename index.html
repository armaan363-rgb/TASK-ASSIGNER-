    Everything a task needs

    Built for servers where who may assign what actually matters.

    👥

    Groups and permission rules

    Make groups from people and roles. Let group A assign to group B, or to one person, with exceptions on both sides. Block people, roles or even @everyone from assigning. Unassign rules work the same way.

    ✅

    Accept or reject

    Assignees get buttons in a DM or the channel, or tasks are accepted directly.

    🖱️

    Interactive everywhere

    Menus, buttons and pop-ups instead of syntax to memorise.

    📅

    Custom due dates

    Pick a preset or type tomorrow 5pm, 3d 4h or 2026-10-20 18:00 +5:30.

    ⏰

    Due reminders

    One button turns them on or off. A heads-up an hour before, and a nudge when it's due.

    See all features

    From idea to done

    Every task follows the same path.

    1. AssignPick people or groups, a priority, a due date and a reminder.
    2. AcceptThe assignee accepts or rejects with a reason.
    3. ProgressUpdates and notes are saved, logged, and reminders keep it on track.
    4. CompleteFinished tasks move into the completed history.

    Commands

    Each one works as a slash command and as a prefix command.

    tasks/assign

    Build a task and assign it

    tasks/unassign

    Browse and unassign tasks

    tasks/tasks

    Browse with Back and Next

    tasks/progress

    Report progress on a task

    tasks/completed

    History and mark complete

    tasks/snooze

    Snooze a task reminder for any time

    todo/todo

    Your personal to-do lists

    todo/todoassign

    Give a member or group a to-do list

    todo/stafftodo

    See the lists you gave out and progress
